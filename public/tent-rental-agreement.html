Section 6

Your responsibilities

  • Use the tent only as intended, with no more than two (2) occupants.
  • Pitch on cleared ground, free of sharp rocks, sticks, coral or glass. A groundsheet or tarp underneath is strongly recommended.
  • Keep flames, stoves, grills, candles, cigarettes, mosquito coils and other heat sources at least 3 metres (10 feet) away. No cooking, smoking or flames inside.
  • Assemble and dismantle poles gently. Fully seat each section and never force or bend them past their natural arc.
  • Open and close zippers slowly, and never pull on the fabric to free a snag.
  • Keep food out of the tent to avoid animal and insect damage.
  • Take the tent down in high winds or severe weather. Tropical storm and hurricane conditions are outside its design limits.
  • Do not machine wash, bleach, use solvents or dry with direct heat.
  • Do not sublet, lend or transfer the tent to anyone outside your party.
  • Report damage, loss or theft to Sight Seers within 24 hours.
  • Return the tent clean, dry and packed in its carry bag with every component.

  • Missing parts or carry bag

Section 9

Replacement terms

9.1 Pole replacement

  • Broken, cracked or missing poles are replaced with the JELUCAMP pole set, Amazon ASIN B0CXT2J7S4, or an equal 7.9 mm fibreglass set if that listing is unavailable.
  • You pay the pole set price plus shipping, import duties and local delivery to Sight Seers.
  • With written approval from Sight Seers, you may supply the correct pole set yourself within fourteen (14) days instead.

9.2 Full tent replacement

  • Full replacement cost is the current retail price of a new, identical JELUCAMP 1 to 2 person dome tent, plus shipping, import duties, taxes and local delivery.
  • If the model is discontinued, the nearest equivalent tent of the same capacity, dimensions, waterproof rating and frame specification is used.
  • Where the tent is fully replaced, any undamaged components returned remain the property of Sight Seers.

9.3 Invoicing

Sight Seers provides an itemised statement with photos and receipts, or current price listings, for any repair or replacement. Charges come out of the deposit first, with any balance due within seven (7) days.
