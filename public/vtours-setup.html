Rehearse before selling a seat

  1. Confirm every line above reads ready.
  2. Start the Mux stream. In OBS use server rtmp://global-live.mux.com:5222/app with your stream key. Wait for Mux to show Active.
  3. Sign the guide in using the Supabase account you created.
  4. Join as a viewer on a second device — a phone on mobile data, not your own laptop. Test what a customer actually gets.
  5. Check the delay. HLS runs 10–30 seconds behind. That is normal, and it is why chat questions lag the picture.
  6. Send a chat message from the viewer. If the “Preview — sample activity” label is still showing, chat is not connected.
  7. Buy a seat with a real card on a session priced at a token amount. Confirm Stripe shows a 200 webhook delivery and the viewer can join afterwards.
  8. Refund the test purchase.

Full instructions, including where to find each credential, are in docs/VTOURS-SETUP.md.
