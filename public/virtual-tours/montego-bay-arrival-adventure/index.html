About This Experience

Landing in a new country is the most uncertain hour of any trip. This live preview session removes every unknown: your guide starts at the Club Mobay VIP gateway inside Sangster International, walking you through exactly what your group sees from jet bridge to lounge to fast-track immigration.

Next, you explore the transfer fleet — the interiors of the luxury vehicles open as spatial 3D views so you can check seating, luggage space, and comfort for your group size. On Apple Vision Pro the vehicle interior opens as a volumetric window you can lean into.

The session closes on a 360° catamaran deck at sunset — look around the bow, the dining area, and the rail where the light lands — so your group can decide on the spot whether to add the charter to your itinerary.

Good to Know

Is Club Mobay included in the price?

This session is the preview and planning experience. Club Mobay passes, transfers, and charters are quoted live during the session and added to your package only if you want them.

We are a group of 20+ — does this still work?

That is exactly who it is for. The planner sizes vehicles, lounge capacity, and catamaran space to your headcount during the session.

What device do I need?

Any browser works. iPhone and iPad get touch-rotate 3D previews; Apple Vision Pro and Quest get the full immersive versions.
