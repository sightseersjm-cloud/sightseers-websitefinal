## How the photos are stored

- Files live in `public/assets/tours/` and are named after the Sight Seers tour title.
- Photos 1,200 px wide or smaller are the original download, unmodified. Larger ones were reduced to 1,200 px wide (JPEG, quality 85) so the page stays light. The table shows the original size.
- "Partial" is the file's own label for a match that shows the place or part of the activity, not the whole tour.
- Two Barbados cruises (Luxury Cruise with Lunch & Open Bar and Luxury Evening Voyage) share one photo.
