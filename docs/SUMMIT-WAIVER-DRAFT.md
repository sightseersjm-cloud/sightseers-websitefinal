## Part C. Waiver and release of liability

**Please read this carefully. By signing you give up certain legal rights.**

**1. The activities.** I am taking part in The Summit Challenge, a timed team event in St. Elizabeth, Jamaica, organised by Sight Seers Caribbean Adventures ("Sight Seers"). It includes a climbing relay, laser tag, an ATV ride, a train tour, team games, and travel by coach, including the connector service, between the meeting point and the venue. I understand some activities are provided by third-party venue operators and guides.

**2. Risks.** I understand these activities involve physical effort and real risks, including falls, collisions, strains, sprains, cuts, bruises, broken bones, heat and sun exposure, insect bites, and, rarely, serious injury or death. Road travel and weather add further risks. I understand these risks cannot be removed completely, even with care, instructions and safety equipment.

**3. My responsibilities.** I will follow the safety briefing and the instructions of the Sight Seers Team Lead, guides and operators. I will use the equipment provided as shown. I will not take part while under the influence of alcohol or drugs. I will wear suitable clothing and closed-toe athletic shoes. I will stop an activity and tell someone if I feel unwell or unsafe. I am fit to take part, or I have told the Team Lead about anything that affects that.

**4. I accept the risks.** I choose to take part and I accept the risks described above.

**5. Release.** To the fullest extent the law allows, I release and agree not to hold Sight Seers, its owners, directors, employees, guides, Team Leads, contractors and agents, and the venue, coach and activity operators it works with (together, "the Released Parties"), responsible for any injury, illness, death, loss, or damage to me or my belongings that happens because of my taking part in The Summit Challenge or the travel connected to it, including injury caused by the ordinary negligence of a Released Party. I also agree not to bring a claim for such loss.

**6. What this does not cover.** This waiver does not limit anyone's liability where the law does not allow it to be limited, including liability for death or personal injury caused by gross negligence or deliberate wrongdoing. [Lawyer to confirm the correct wording for Jamaican law.]

**7. Emergency treatment.** If I am injured or become ill, I allow Sight Seers and the Team Lead to arrange first aid and emergency medical care for me, and I will be responsible for the cost of any treatment or transport that is not paid by someone else. Sight Seers is not a medical provider. [Confirm whether the venue or event insurance covers participants.]

**8. Insurance.** I understand that I am responsible for my own health and accident insurance. [Confirm what, if any, cover Sight Seers or the venue holds.]

**9. Photos and video.** I allow Sight Seers to photograph and film the event and to use images of me to promote its services. [Optional box: I do not agree to this.]

**10. Equipment and conduct.** I will take care of equipment I am given. I may be asked to leave an activity, without a refund, if my behaviour puts myself or others at risk.

**11. My details.** The information I have given in this form is used to run the event and keep me safe. Sight Seers keeps it only as long as it needs to and does not sell it. [Confirm retention period and privacy wording.]

**12. Governing law.** This waiver is governed by the laws of Jamaica. [Lawyer to confirm.]

**13. Understanding.** I have read this waiver, I understand it, and I sign it freely. I am 18 or older. [If under 18, a parent or guardian must sign for me. Confirm whether minors are allowed on this event.]

## Notes for you and your lawyer
- **"Not liable" has limits.** Courts in many places will not enforce a release that tries to cover gross negligence, or that the signer did not clearly understand. Clause 6 is there for that reason. A lawyer should decide what is enforceable in Jamaica.
- **Third parties.** Clause 5 includes the venue and coach operators. Check that your contracts with them agree, and whether they need their own waiver.
- **Insurance.** A waiver does not replace public liability and accident insurance. Clauses 7 and 8 need your real position filled in.
- **Facts used.** The activities, the St. Elizabeth location, the Mandeville coach, the Kingston connector, the Team Lead role, the shirt choice (Team Green and Team Orange) and the checklist all come from your proposal. Nothing else was added.
